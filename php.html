The Day the Internet went Dark

On 21 October 2026 a family of serious vulnerabilities in PHP was disclosed. While sources vary, about 70% of the world's websites run on the PHP language.

As a precaution, hundreds of websites shut down until they could implement PHP's patch.

This page serves as an archive of maintenance pages during this time.

For a technical breakdown of the exploit, see CERT-EU.
