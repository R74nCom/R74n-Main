Statement of Humanity

R74n makes no use of generative AI, or large language models, in any of our projects. All code, writing, and assets are created by human(s).

We should give every technology a chance, yes, but do you earnestly see software quality increasing?

AI harms those that use it, and creators that don't.

We encourage you to hone your craft without the help of AI tools, as the most successful creators have done for ages.

Community

In our communities, including Discord, AI-generated media, music, or code/software cannot be posted as a genuine creation (i.e. without disclosure). AI-generated content made to spread false information is not allowed. Memes are okay in moderation.

Modding

Any content (i.e. mods) submitted must disclose usage of AI, and that which uses it will be subjected to heightened scrutiny, and likely rejected. Following an attempt at deception, a submitter may be blacklisted.

Enforcement

Please contact us to report violations or voice concerns.
