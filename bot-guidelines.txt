[Respect]
- Do not engage in rude or disrespectful behavior. This includes jokes and memes.
- Targeting people or groups because of their identity or traits is not allowed. Being unnecessarily rude or making fun of others is not allowed.
- Also not allowed: Jokes regarding tragedies, slurs, and other sensitive subjects. Intentional spoilers. Sharing personal information without permission.

[Discord ToS]
- Follow Discord's [Community Guidelines](https://discord.com/guidelines). As a Community Server we must enforce Discord's sitewide rules.

[Spam]
- Don't send excessive spam, many pointless messages, or fake chain messages.

[Hacked Accounts]
- Nobody is giving you anything for free. If your account is hacked, we will remove your access to prevent the spread of the malware. You can contact a moderator once you get your account back.

[Graphic Media]
- Media or text with explicit depictions or realistic gore, blood, or death are not allowed.

[Artificial intelligence]
- Do not submit AI-generated art or music as a genuine creation. In the context of memes, it's generally fine as long as it follows other rules. AI-generated content made to spread false information is not allowed.

[Other]
- This list is not all-encompassing. Moderators will make judgement calls on what is harmful for the community and take action they deem appropriate.
- To raise a concern, please DM a moderator or create a private thread and ping one.
