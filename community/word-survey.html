Word Survey

In May 2026, we asked the community a question to help jump start the Wordslop dataset:

"Enter 10 words that are as far apart in meaning from each other as possible, comma-separated."

The lists of words distinct from each other helped us get a diverse collection of words to base the minigame's groups off of.

We have removed spam and inappropriate words, corrected typos, and analyzed the data.

Spoiler warning: Naturally, this page includes many words and groups from Wordslop.
